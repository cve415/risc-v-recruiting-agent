## Role Tracks

The agent classifies candidates across a structured set of role tracks spanning both lanes — from deep silicon engineering (design, verification, software/toolchain, functional safety, security) to commercial go-to-market (field applications, IP sales, ecosystem and partnerships). Each track carries its own evaluation rubric calibrated to the signals that actually predict success in that role. Track definitions are part of the proprietary calibration layer.

## Agentic Augmentation Approach

The stack offloads high-volume, low-entropy discovery to AI so the human lead focuses on high-stakes relationships and deal closing.

| AI agent (high-volume discovery) | Human lead (high-stakes relationship) |
|----------------------------------|----------------------------------------|
| Multi-silo signal detection (GitHub, arXiv, RISC-V Summit proceedings) | Empathy, cultural fit, motivation assessment |
| Technical vetting (ISA depth, codebase quality, toolchain mastery) | Final negotiation (equity, comp, relocation) |
| Market mapping (competitor intel, talent-density heatmaps by region) | Strategic advisory (long-term engineering partnerships) |

## Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)

All AI-generated technical theses are reviewed by the lead before outreach. No autonomous send.

1. **Validation** — Human reviews every scored thesis before any candidate contact
2. **Ethics guardrails** — Mandatory diversity and compliance check before outreach
3. **Correction loop** — Human feedback on scoring accuracy refines role rubrics over time
4. **Minimum evidence bar** — 2 independent markers required before a candidate is scored (e.g. specific commit + technical post)
