# RISC-V Academic Ecosystem — Philippines

**Compiled By:** Christopher Velasco, RISC-V International Academia & Training SIG member  
**Source:** CHED curriculum mandates (CMO 25/17), DOST-ERDT consortium records, faculty research profiles.

The Philippine engineering and CS ecosystem is **early-stage** relative to RISC-V adoption. Tier 1 entries are sparse. Most strong programs represent Tier 2/3 opportunities for curriculum development and partnership outreach. The **DOST-ERDT consortium** (UP Diliman, DLSU, AdDU, MSU-IIT, and others) is the fastest path to Tier 1 adoption.

**Key community contact:** Christopher Misola attempted to launch a RISC-V meetup in the Philippines 5+ years ago — one of the earliest RISC-V community organizers in the country. Current role: Teaching Fellow, Executive Course on Emerging Technologies and Anticipatory Governance (ECETAG), Pasig. Natural co-organizer for a revived Philippines RISC-V chapter and credible internal advocate at CHED/DOST level.

## Tier 1 — Confirmed Coursework or Lab Use

### University of the Philippines Diliman (UP Diliman) — Quezon City, NCR
Strongest Philippine institution with documented RISC-V exposure through architecture research and open-ISA coursework. Microelectronics and Microprocessors Lab in ECE. Verify against ECE 150 / EEE 158 course syllabi. As UP System anchor, curriculum decisions here propagate across UP Diliman, Los Baños, Manila, and Visayas campuses.
- **Role routing:** Role 1 (Silicon Architecture), Role 2 (DV/Verification)
- **GitHub signals:** ECE student RISC-V pipeline simulator repos, UP open-hardware projects
- **Sourcing thesis:** UP Diliman ECE + DOST-ERDT fellowship = Philippines' strongest engineering graduate signal

## Tier 2 — Active Teaching Materials or Project-Based Use

### NCR (National Capital Region)
- **De La Salle University (DLSU) — Manila** — Strong ECE and CS; embedded systems and computer organization courses; DOST-ERDT member. Role 1/2.
- **Mapúa University — Manila/Makati** — Engineering-focused; ECE and CE; practical lab culture aligns with RISC-V FPGA integration. Role 1.
- **TUP (Technological University of the Philippines) — Manila** — National polytechnic; ECE/EE programs; cost-effective open-hardware lab adoption fit. Role 1.
- **PUP (Polytechnic University of the Philippines) — Manila** — Large public polytechnic; CS and ECE offerings. Role 1.

### Region III (Central Luzon)
- **Bulacan State University (BulSU) — Malolos** — State university; engineering and IT programs. Role 1.
- **Nueva Ecija University of Science and Technology (NEUST) — Cabanatuan** — STEM mission alignment with RISC-V lab tracks. Role 1.

### Region IV-A (CALABARZON)
- **Batangas State University (BatStateU)** — Larger state university in Luzon; ECE/EE with architecture/embedded tracks. Role 1.

### Region VII (Central Visayas)
- **University of San Carlos (USC) — Cebu City** — Most research-active private university in Visayas; strong ECE/CE; likely early Visayas adopter. Role 1.
- **Cebu Institute of Technology University (CIT-U)** — Engineering-focused; FPGA-based RISC-V labs would complement practical curriculum. Role 1.

### Mindanao
- **MSU-IIT (Mindanao State University – Iligan Institute of Technology) — Iligan City** — Strongest engineering school in Mindanao; ECE and CS research-active; DOST-ERDT member. Most likely Mindanao institution to achieve Tier 1 status. Role 1/2.
- **USTP (Cagayan de Oro)** — CHED-designated Science and Technology university; strong engineering mandate. Role 1.
- **USeP (Davao City)** — State university with ECE and CS programs. Role 1.
- **Ateneo de Davao University — Davao City** — CS and engineering; research-active with Davao tech sector links. Role 1.
- **UP Mindanao (Davao City)** — UP System campus; research-active; likely to follow UP Diliman lead. Role 1.

## Philippines Sourcing Context

- **CHED CMO alignment:** CHED CMO No. 25 (ECE curriculum) and CMO No. 17 (Computer Engineering) define required architecture content. RISC-V curriculum pitches that map explicitly to these CMOs have the highest adoption probability.
- **DOST-ERDT network:** The Engineering Research and Development for Technology consortium (UP Diliman, DLSU, AdDU, MSU-IIT, and others) is the fastest path to Tier 1 adoption — ERDT faculty are most likely to introduce open-ISA tools into graduate and advanced undergraduate courses.
- **UP System cluster:** Treat as a propagation cluster — Diliman decisions cascade to Los Baños, Manila, Visayas, and Mindanao campuses. One champion at UP Diliman = national reach.
- **MSU-IIT as Mindanao anchor:** If one Mindanao school achieves Tier 1, it is most likely MSU-IIT. Prioritize outreach here for Mindanao-region pipeline.
- **Current pipeline reality:** Philippines talent is primarily a **Lane B transition pipeline** at this stage — software engineers and embedded developers who can be guided toward RISC-V. Expect Lane A (native RISC-V) candidates to emerge from UP Diliman and MSU-IIT first.
