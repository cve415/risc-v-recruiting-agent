## International Institutions

*See regional files listed in the Overview above. The brief entries below are retained for inline reference; the regional files contain full tier breakdowns, GitHub signals, and sourcing notes.*

### Europe

- **ETH Zurich** *(Switzerland)* – Named in the RISC-V International Annual Report 2025. Research groups in computer architecture and hardware security are active RISC-V adopters; outputs include custom RISC-V SoC tapeouts and open-source RTL. Primary talent signal: SystemVerilog/VHDL repos, CVA6/Ariane forks, low-power embedded RISC-V work. Recruiting thesis: strong pipeline for Silicon Architecture (Role 1) and DV/Formal (Role 2).

- **University of Bologna** *(Italy)* – Named in the Annual Report. Home of the PULP Platform (Parallel Ultra-Low-Power), one of the most-forked open RISC-V SoC projects globally. Graduates routinely contribute to OpenHW Group cores (CV32E40P, CVA6). Primary talent signal: PULP SDK, Hero, Mr. Wolf, GreenDroid repos. Recruiting thesis: deep embedded and energy-efficient AI accelerator bench strength (Role 1, Role 3).

- **Barcelona Supercomputing Center / EuroHPC** *(Spain)* – Named in the Annual Report under the DARE (Deployment of RISC-V in Europe) initiative. BSC leads the European RISC-V HPC push including the Lagarto/Tristan processor program. Primary talent signal: OpenPiton, Lagarto repos, MPI/OpenMP RISC-V HPC work. Recruiting thesis: HPC track (Role 3) and transition candidates from European x86/ARM HPC clusters.

- **KU Leuven** *(Belgium)* – IMEC affiliate with active RISC-V security and TEE research. Strong output in confidential computing and post-quantum crypto on RISC-V. Recruiting thesis: Role 8 (Security/Confidential Computing).

- **TU Delft** *(Netherlands)* – RISC-V in embedded systems and FPGA courses; active AMBA/AXI interconnect research. Recruiting thesis: Role 1 (Silicon Architecture), bring-up profiles.

### Latin America

- **University of São Paulo (USP)** *(Brazil)* – Presented at the RISC-V Brazil Event (Campinas, July 2025). Research in RISC-V SoC design for IoT and edge AI. Primary talent signal: SystemVerilog tapeout projects, Chipyard-based SoC repos. Recruiting thesis: embedded SoC (Role 1) and IoT vertical pipeline.

- **University of Brasília (UnB)** *(Brazil)* – Presented at the RISC-V Brazil Event (Campinas, July 2025). Computer architecture curriculum built around RISC-V; active in FPGA-based processor labs. Recruiting thesis: Role 1 and Role 2 entry-level pipeline from a fast-growing RISC-V adoption region.

- **Unicamp – University of Campinas** *(Brazil)* – Host institution for the RISC-V Brazil Event (July 2025). Strong EE/CS research output; known for embedded systems and hardware security groups. Recruiting thesis: Role 1, Role 8; bridge to Brazil's growing semiconductor talent base (RISC-V Brazil initiative).

### Asia-Pacific

- **IIT Madras** *(India)* – Developed Shakti, one of India's first production-grade open RISC-V processor families. Several Shakti variants tapeout at TSMC/Intel Foundry. Primary talent signal: Shakti repo forks, bluespec BSV, RISC-V Linux bring-up. Recruiting thesis: Role 1 (deep microarchitecture), strong transition profiles for India-based hiring.

- **National Taiwan University (NTU)** *(Taiwan)* – Active RISC-V SoC design courses; proximity to TSMC supply chain. Research in VLSI and custom accelerators on RISC-V. Recruiting thesis: Role 1 and Role 3 near TSMC/MediaTek talent cluster.

- **Tsinghua University / Peking University** *(China)* – Among the most active RISC-V research producers globally; strong overlap with T-Head (Alibaba) and BeiSi (SOPHGO) ecosystem. Note: export-control screening required for US-regulated roles. Recruiting thesis: Role 1, Role 3 — with appropriate compliance review for restricted technology.

## Sourcing Notes for International Profiles

- **Language of repos**: European and Latin American contributors often mix English/native-language READMEs — do not penalize for non-English documentation.
- **Degree equivalency**: Bologna Process (EU) means a 3-year Laurea Triennale + 2-year Laurea Magistrale = US BS + MS equivalent. Do not discount European candidates for "only" having a Laurea.
- **PULP/CVA6 affiliation**: any contributor to the PULP Platform or CVA6 repo should be treated as a strong Lane A signal regardless of university affiliation.
- **RISC-V Brazil pipeline**: the July 2025 Campinas event confirmed institutional momentum — USP/UnB/Unicamp graduates are now entering the job market with native RISC-V curricula, not just electives.
