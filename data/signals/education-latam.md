# RISC-V Academic Ecosystem — Latin America

**Compiled By:** Christopher Velasco, RISC-V International Academia & Training SIG member  
**Source:** Faculty Lattes CVs (Brazil), Google Scholar profiles, RISC-V Brazil Event (Campinas, July 2025) presenters, course catalog records.

Latin America's RISC-V academic footprint is concentrated in Brazil, with emerging activity in Mexico, Chile, Colombia, and Argentina. Brazil anchors the region — federal research university funding (CNPq/CAPES) and the ChipBR national initiative have supported processor architecture research intersecting with RISC-V. No formal LATAM-wide RISC-V curriculum consortium exists yet — this is an open organizational opportunity.

---

## Tier 1 — Confirmed Named Programs or Production Tapeouts

### UFRGS (Universidade Federal do Rio Grande do Sul) — Porto Alegre, Brazil
**Strongest documented RISC-V program in Latin America.** Instituto de Informática — Computer Architecture Group (GAPH) has published peer-reviewed RISC-V microarchitecture and NoC research. MC422/computer organization uses *P&H RISC-V Edition*. Student and graduate RISC-V core implementations on public repos.
- **Role routing:** Role 1 (Silicon Architecture), Role 2 (DV/Formal)
- **GitHub signals:** GAPH-PUCRS org, RISC-V pipeline repos, NoC-RISC-V
- **Sourcing thesis:** UFRGS GAPH alumni = Brazil's strongest Lane A signal; treat as equivalent to US Tier 1 architecture program

### UNICAMP (Universidade Estadual de Campinas) — Campinas, São Paulo, Brazil
MC722 (Computer Organization and Architecture) and graduate courses reference RISC-V. Faculty publications include RISC-V accelerator and memory hierarchy research. Strong industry connections to Campinas's semiconductor design cluster (Texas Instruments, Eldorado Research Institute). Host institution of the **RISC-V Brazil Event (Campinas, July 2025)** — confirming institutional RISC-V leadership in Brazil.
- **Role routing:** Role 1, Role 3 (AI/ML — accelerator research)
- **GitHub signals:** IC-Unicamp org, RISC-V accelerator repos, FEEC chip design projects

### USP (Universidade de São Paulo) — São Paulo, Brazil
Brazil's largest and highest-ranked university. Escola Politécnica (POLI) architecture sequences have moved toward RISC-V from MIPS via P&H textbook transition. PCS3616 and adjacent courses. Presented at RISC-V Brazil Event (Campinas, July 2025). USP adoption propagates to affiliated and benchmarking institutions across Brazil.
- **Role routing:** Role 1, Role 4 (Toolchain/Software)
- **GitHub signals:** USP-RISC-V student repos, Lattes-indexed faculty RISC-V publications

### ITA (Instituto Tecnológico de Aeronáutica) — São José dos Campos, Brazil
Brazil's most selective engineering institution (military technical university). Computer Architecture and Digital Systems coursework uses RISC-V in advanced tracks. Research in embedded and safety-critical systems intersects with RISC-V's open, verifiable ISA properties. ITA graduates enter Embraer, AEB (Brazilian Space Agency), and defense electronics.
- **Role routing:** Role 7 (FuSa/Safety-Critical — space/defense output), Role 1
- **GitHub signals:** RISC-V safety-critical embedded repos, aerospace embedded systems projects
- **Sourcing thesis:** ITA candidate with space or defense background = direct input to space vertical (Frontgrade Gaisler transition track)

### UnB (Universidade de Brasília) — Brasília, Brazil
Federal capital university. Computer architecture sequence moving toward RISC-V. Proximity to government ICT policy creates alignment with Brazil's semiconductor and open-hardware priorities. Presented at RISC-V Brazil Event (Campinas, July 2025).
- **Role routing:** Role 1, Role 5 (GTM/Ecosystem — policy-adjacent output)
- **GitHub signals:** CIC-UnB RISC-V student projects, architecture lab repos

- **UFRJ / COPPE (Rio de Janeiro)** — Graduate engineering; RISC-V in processor architecture research. Strong industry connections via COPPE. Role 1.

### Mexico

### Chile

### Colombia

## LATAM Sourcing Context

- **Brazil dominates Tier 1:** UFRGS GAPH, UNICAMP, USP, and ITA form the core LATAM pipeline — analogous to India's IIT cluster. ChipBR national initiative is creating additional upstream pressure.
- **RISC-V Brazil Event (July 2025, Campinas):** USP, UnB, Unicamp all presented. Graduates from these programs are entering the job market now with native RISC-V curricula. Pipeline is live.
- **No LATAM-wide consortium:** Unlike Europe (PULP) or India (NPTEL), no formal LATAM RISC-V academic network exists — this is an opportunity for first-mover ecosystem relationships.
- **Mexico's manufacturing corridor:** Guadalajara ("Mexico's Silicon Valley") and Monterrey's growing semiconductor supply chain create industry pull; CINVESTAV Guadalajara and Tec de Monterrey are best positioned.
- **Verification path (Brazil):** Cross-reference faculty Lattes CVs at lattes.cnpq.br for RISC-V publications. For Mexican/Andean institutions, check course catalogs and Google Scholar for ISA-specific architecture papers.
- **Textbook transition indicator:** P&H RISC-V edition adoption is the fastest proxy for RISC-V coursework across all these countries — where Lattes-indexed faculty cite it, coursework adoption follows.
